# Do Over

A planner for ADHD adults, built around the moment every planner dies: the day falling apart. You say what happened in one sentence, it re-triages the rest of your day and shows you a diff, and nothing applies until you accept it. No streaks, no red overdue wall.

**Status (Sep 2026):** research and spec done, landing page live, build not started. There is a public go/no-go gate before any real engineering happens. Site: https://getdoover.com

## What is in this repo

- **[FAILURE-CASES.md](FAILURE-CASES.md)**, the failure ledger. Real ways planners have failed, each turned into a rule the engine must obey and a test it must pass. This is the acceptance suite. It grows every time someone tells me how their planner died.
- **[BUILD-LOG.md](BUILD-LOG.md)**, dated build updates, including the parts that did not work.
- `site/`, the landing page. `netlify/`, the one function that sends the waitlist confirmation.

## What I am asking for

Two things. Roast the landing page. And tell me the exact moment you quit your last planner, ADHD or not. Open an issue, or reply wherever you found this. One sentence is enough.
